Biomes:

Distinct geographical ecosystems, known as biomes, can help broadly estimate the location.

Grasslands:
Split into sub-categories of tropical and temperate, grasslands are characterized by flat land and grass. Well known grasslands include the Pampas (South America), Veldt (South Africa), Steppe (Eurasia), and Great Plains (US).

Rainforests:
Like grasslands, they are also split into tropical rainforests and temperate rainforests. They are characterized by high humidity, layers of vegetation, and (sometimes) tropical plants.

Deserts:
Dry, arid, sandy. Plant life is naturally scarce and mainly consists of cacti and shrubs. Well known deserts include the Sahara, Arabian, and Chihuahuan Deserts.

Agriculture:

Biomes are great, but can't be efficiently applied to less remote areas. For example, farmland can easily alter the vegetation and throw off those locating soley based on biomes. This is why it's also important to get famillar with agriculture. The most common crops in the world are sugarcane, corn, rice, and wheat. These, along with some other ones, are discussed below!

Sugarcane, with 1.98 billion metric tons produced worldwide, is first in crop production. After harvestation, it is made into biofuel, sugar, and molasses. Places with a prominent sugarcane fields include São Paulo, Brazil; Queensland, Australia; Philippines; and Java, Indonesia.

Misc Metas:

Eucalyptus trees often grow on Australian roadsides. These trees have a distinct bark that sheds and leaves a white, smooth under-bark

Terraces are stair-shaped farmland that helps with irrigation and prevents erosion. They are mainly located in East and Southeasy Asia and Peru.
